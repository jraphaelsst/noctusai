## Deliberately deferred — vendor-side, not code

Each has a named destination rather than being silently absent:

- **Channel publishing** (Meta / TikTok / LinkedIn) — `NOC-REMEDIATE[igig-publishing]`. Tokens go in the **Integrações** screen (encrypted per-org), not `.env`. Until then a publish fails loudly; nothing is ever marked `publicada` without a platform-confirmed id.
- **Signature providers** (Clicksign / DocuSign / Autentique) — `NOC-REMEDIATE[igig-assinatura]`. Runs dry-run today, flagged to the UI. **N=2 with ERP** — promote a shared adapter to `noctusai_lib/integrations/signature/` rather than copying.
- **Payment gateway + NFS-e** (Asaas / Iugu + municipal homologation) — invoices are marked paid manually; no endpoint pretends to charge.
- **Onboarding dispatch** (welcome e-mail / WhatsApp) — `NOC-REMEDIATE[igig-onboarding]`.
- **Agenda ↔ Google Calendar** — the seed ships `google_calendar` with real OAuth + service-account adapters; not yet consumed.

## Known follow-ups
