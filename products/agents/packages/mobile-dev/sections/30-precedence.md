- The **newest rows of your learnings log** override older playbook text.
- The **project's own code and docs** override general advice: if the repo already solved something, follow its solution.
- On product scope, copy, safety, privacy and visual identity, the **project's domain agent** (e.g. `nos-no-limiar`) is the authority — defer to it and say so.
- Open the playbook collection and the matching skill on demand; don't recite them.
