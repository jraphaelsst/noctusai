# Social Wiring

The real-estate operations hub of noc: lead intake, the sales funnel and cards,
properties and owners, contracts and certificates, marketing, and the WhatsApp
transport for **Julia**, the SDR agent. One seed-factory, single-container
product (backend 8011, frontend 8160, Supabase schema `social_wiring`).

Social Wiring is the **transport + CRM + operations** side. The agent's brain
(prompt, tools loop, LLM keys) is hosted in the `agents` product (Agent Studio)
and consumed over `/api/channels/*`; SW never calls an LLM for Julia's turns.

## Stack

- **Backend**: FastAPI via `create_product_app()` from `noctusai_seed`; features are product modules assembled from the `MODULES` list in `app/main.py` (`app/modules/*`) plus the Julia routers in `app/routers/julia_*`.
- **Frontend**: React via `createProductApp()` + `createProductLayout()` from `@noctusai/seed`; nav in `src/nav/navGroups.ts` (Julia group in `src/nav/julia.ts`).
- **Database**: Supabase, schema `social_wiring`; RLS own-org + service role.
- **Auth**: SSO + direct login (seed factory); `/chat` and the Julia privacy notice (`/privacidade/:slug`) are public.
- **Container**: ONE container — uvicorn serves the built SPA + API on one port (`serve_spa`).

## The Julia module

Julia is the WhatsApp SDR for a real-estate org (renamed from "Lais" on
2026-10-19; Lais is Lastro's product, studied in a private study outside git).

- **Julia is hosted in agents** (julia-v2): her prompt, knowledge, qualification standard, controls (ligado/drenando/desligado, per-conversation pause) and the shared lead thread all live there; SW is transport + CRM + operations and consumes her **through the API only** (`app/clients/agents.py`). SW never gates on a cached copy of a control: agents decides on every turn and answers with a `status`; any non-served status, error or timeout sends nothing (fail closed).
- **Opt-outs and data requests** (owner 2026-10-21): the lead opt-out is AGENTS' per-org record; SW shows it at **Julia > Opt-outs** (`/julia/opt-outs`, `app/routers/julia_opt_outs_router.py`, `julia_opt_out_service`): history, "Reenviar notificação", "Reativar atendimento" and "Marcar como não contatar" (admin, reason required), and a non-blocking warning for the human operator in Conversas. **No lead message can delete or change anything**: the keyword auto-erase is gone; Julia registers a data-subject request through her tool (`POST /api/julia/ferramentas/solicitacao-titular` -> `julia_solicitacoes_titular`, 15-day deadline), an admin decides it, and an erasure is the admin's "apagar-dados" act (keeper `tests/test_lead_path_never_deletes.py`).
- **Public-number protection** (owner 2026-10-21, GUARDRAILS B2/B5/B8/B10): the org **staff list** (`julia_staff`, **Julia > Equipe do WhatsApp**, `julia_equipe_whatsapp_router`, `julia_staff_service`) is the ONE place staff is decided, by the sender's NUMBER only: any number not on it is a lead, always; an operator-role number keeps the One Chat path (the connection's `authorized_numbers` is now its mirror, written by the connection PATCH through `sincronizar_de_lista`); every other staff role never enters the lead flow (message stored, admins noticed once an hour); the human lines SW appends to an agents thread go on the staff append route with `role: "humano"`, `autor {nome, papel}` and `autor_user_id`. **Julia > Proteção** (`julia_protecao_router`, `julia_protecao_service`, migration 298) holds every limit per org with its default (per-number inbound rate 20/60 s before agents is called; media AI budget 10/chat/day and 200/line/day; the automatic temporary block after 3 abuse signals in 60 min for 24 h, admins notified), the per-org **blocklist** (manual with reason/who/when, or automatic-temporary; a blocked number's inbound is recorded and never reaches agents or an AI call) and the operator-editable lead document prompt. A lead's media travels STRUCTURED (`messages[i].midia {tipo, texto_extraido <= 2000, legenda?}`, never inlined in `texto`) and is resolved under the lead caps (`MediaService.resolve_inbound_lead`: streamed size caps 16 MB/5 min audio, 8 MB image, 10 MB/3-page PDF; no video; no redirects; the WAHA key only to the WAHA host; CPF/RG/CNH masked by `identidade_mascara`); after agents registers the identification SW masks the typed numbers in its stored copy (`MessageStore.redigir_identificacao`) and in the chat's `whatsapp_chats.last_message_preview` while it is still that message's preview (`WhatsAppChatStore.redigir_preview`; each step separate, a failure logged by type only, never blocking the reply) and purges that chat's `message.new` / `chat.upsert` carrying one from the inbox stream's retained window (`bus.redact`; the value masking is the lib's `identificador_redacao`). The Julia/media modules log no text, no raw exception and no bare chat id/LID (`ref_log`; keeper `tests/test_julia_log_hygiene.py`). A 429 `capacidade` that outlasts the re-queues goes to a human, never dropped.
- **Turn flow**: WAHA webhook → `services/julia_lead_turn_service.py` → agents `POST /api/channels/turns` (studio key `julia`, formerly `lead-imob`; the request carries the lead ref, `gatilho` and a data-only `contexto`) → on a served turn the bubbles are sent and the typed actions (`handoff`, `lead_pronto`, `intencao_visita`, `captacao`, `encerrar`, …) are applied by `julia_acoes_service`; every outbound WhatsApp send passes the single gate `julia_envio_gate`. A lead who asked to stop holds an agents-side lead-level opt-out (`encerrar` + `lead_pediu_parar` / `lead_opt_out: true` means agents already recorded it and notified the admins): SW then stops that lead's follow-ups (`gen + 1`, timeline event `lead_opt_out`), and every send that does not go through an agents turn (regua template, Meta first contact, review ask, disparo, visit notice) re-reads agents' consent state and refuses, leaving an `envio_bloqueado` event (`julia_envio_gate.exigir_sem_opt_out`; unreadable state refuses too). A human attendant's outbound and a lead message agents declined to answer are appended to the thread without a turn (`julia_thread_service`).
- **Mirrors, not sources**: `whatsapp_chats.agente_thread_id/agente_estado/agente_pausado` and `julia_contatos.slots/operacao` (+ `ai_outputs` stage history) mirror agents' state, written only from agents' answers. The CRM send fires on the stage agents COMPUTED (`julia_envio_regras.estagio_minimo`) or the `lead_pronto` action. The hand-off note (profile + atendimento summary + masked identity) goes to the org owner by default.
- **Tools for the agent**: agents calls back `POST /api/julia/ferramentas/{nome}` with a product token (scope `social-wiring:lais-tools` — kept under its old spelling until both products rename it). Tools: `buscar_imoveis` (structured query, `similar_a`, `excluir_codigos`, `limite` 1..5, one-axis-at-a-time widening reported as `relaxado`, `restantes_estimados`; standard item keys — `itens_essenciais` / `caracteristicas` / `itens_desejaveis` — map to catalogue predicates via `ITENS_PADRAO` in `julia_busca_imoveis.py` (e.g. `jardim_quintal` = quintal/jardim but not jardiminverno, `terreo_sem_escadas` = a house with `pavimentos = 1`); items the catalogue cannot verify (pets, proximidades, terreo on a non-house) never filter or rank and come back as `itens_nao_verificaveis`), `imovel_campanha`, `detalhes_imovel`, `horarios_visita`, `consultar_empreendimento`. Stage list for the Envio comparator: `GET /api/julia/qualificacao/estagios` (proxy of agents).
- **Pages** (`/julia/*`): Painel, Conversas, Configurações, Agente (line routing only), Conhecimento + Perguntas (read-only notices: moved to the agents app), Visitas, Reengajamento (réguas), Notificações, Envio, Leads, Equipes, Modelos, Disparos, Proprietários, Relatórios, Captação, Avaliações, Crédito. Old `/lais/*` URLs redirect.
- **Julia frontend (julia-v2)**: Julia lives in the agents app and SW only consumes her over the API, so SW's FE holds no behaviour, prompt, rule or `dica` text. `/julia/agente` is LINE ROUTING (`GET /api/julia/roteamento`, `PUT /api/julia/roteamento/linhas/{id}`: "esta linha atende leads com a Julia" per WhatsApp line + the hand-off notification target, default the org owner) plus the agents-link status, the privacy notice (legal, stays in SW) and the link "Configurar a Julia no app de agentes". The Conversas thread header shows Julia's state exactly as agents reports it (`GET .../chats/{chat}/agente`: ativo / pausado / concluído / transferido with a `fonte` flag `agents` | `espelho` (stale) | `nenhuma`), the computed-stage chip (label + score only) and the pause/resume toggle (a proxy to agents). The `handoff` notification renders profile, summary and MASKED identity (never a raw CPF/RG; `lib/mascaraIdentidade.ts` re-masks defensively). Envio's rule is one `estagio_minimo` dropdown per operation fed by the ordered stages of agents' standard (`GET /api/julia/qualificacao/estagios`, label + order only); the retired `grupos` is never sent (the server answers 422). `/julia/perguntas` and `/julia/conhecimento` are read-only "agora é configurado no app de agentes" notices (routes kept for bookmarks; the stored data is not deleted).
- **Atendimento in blocks** (`app/modules/atendimento_blocos`, `/atendimento-blocos`): SDR (Julia) → atendimento → negociação → jurídico → financeiro → contador, with recorded handoffs.
- **Maya supervision** (`app/modules/supervisao`, `/supervisao`): nightly scorecards per block (Julia's rubric `sdr.v2`: first response is split agent vs human, only human acts take a handoff over, an accept with no actor still records its juridico handoff), handoff sweep, owner-only daily report; a supervision hook that fails raises a visible owner alert (`supervisao_falha`) and flags the day `dados_incompletos`.
- **Julia ↔ agents contract**: the per-turn `contexto` SW renders is DATA ONLY and carries only the keys agents' schema accepts (`julia_contexto_service.ESTADO_CHAVES_AGENTS`; `tests/services/test_agents_contract_v2.py` validates it against agents' `channels.v2.json`); no prompt text, knowledge, question list or tool list is sent. A 422 from agents is an integration bug and raises an owner alert (`agents_contrato_invalido`). The `/julia/agente` page backend (`/api/julia/roteamento`) is **line routing only** (which numbers feed the agent + the hand-off target), white-label per org; behaviour is configured in agents. The one-time knowledge copy into agents is `python -m app.jobs.julia_conhecimento_backfill` (dry-run by default).
- **Data**: tables `social_wiring.julia_*` (migration 287 renamed them from `lais_*`). Temporary compatibility views `lais_*` remain for the previous image — `NOC-REMEDIATE[drop-lais-compat-views]`. Old `/api/lais/*` URLs are served by a path alias until the next release.
- **Browser access (migration 289)**: every `julia_*` table and `lais_*` view is **service-role only** — no anon/authenticated grant or policy, default privileges revoked for future tables, and every `social_wiring` view runs `security_invoker`. All reads go through the backend API (A7 rules in `julia_acesso`). DB probes `julia_lockdown.*` gate predeploy.

## Other modules

`leads`, `pipeline` (funil + processos de venda), `card_hub`, `portal_leads` (portal ingestion + ROI), `imovel_hub`, `condominios`, `permutas`, `empresas`, `matriculas`, `certidoes`, `agentes_financeiros`, `meta_ads`, `instagram`, `simec`, `media_creation` (CoreStudio), `transcricoes`, `edicao_fotos`, `email_marketing`, `mailchimp`, `scheduling`, `youtube`, `n8n`.

- **Contract specifications** (migration 308, owner 2026-10-24; contract `projects/sw-lead-to-contract/CONTRACT.md` §10): the kind of contract is a SELECTABLE set of facets from the code catalogue `contrato_gerador/especificacoes.py` (`compra_venda` base; pagamento `a_vista` · `sinal_saldo` · `financiamento_bancario` · `fgts` · `parcelas_diretas` · `intermediarias`; `confissao_divida`; `permuta`; `intermediacao`), each declaring the readiness `falta` keys it asks for. `PUT /api/clientes/{cliente_id}/contratos/{contrato_id}/especificacoes` stores it (`atendimento_contratos.especificacoes` + `especificacoes_versao`; NULL = legacy mode, the derived switches); `sugerir(switches)` is the data-derived suggestion and `derivacao.modelo_derivado` reads off it. Open owner question G1 (à vista without sinal → multa rescisória base) is the pending requisito `negociacao.multa_rescisoria_base`. The selector lives in the Contratos tab ("Gerar contrato" → `components/card/EspecificacoesPicker.tsx`): grouped by eixo, `requer`/`exclusiva_com` enforced in the UI (disabled + reason), pre-filled from the readiness report's `sugeridas` ("Aplicar sugestão"), `conflitos` shown as bloqueios, a legacy contract saved only on "Usar especificações"; the FE catalogue is the GENERATED `frontend/src/lib/especificacoesCatalogo.json`, pinned to `CATALOGO` by `backend/tests/modules/card_hub/test_especificacoes_catalogo_fe.py` (`NOCTUS_REGENERAR_CATALOGO_FE=1` regenerates). The Negociação panel hides the Intermediários subtab when no live contract selected `intermediacao` and it has no rows (rows outside the selection stay, flagged "fora das especificações"). The readiness scoping is the next slice.
- **Certidões grid: manual cells and re-reads** (migration 302): a hand-edited cell (`resultado_origem='manual'`) is locked against "Reler" and the Crednet; `POST /api/certidoes/resultados/{id}/voltar-automatico` ("Voltar à leitura automática") clears the override and re-runs the cell's automatic source (stored PDF, Crednet for serasa, registry response). A CENPROT/Serasa consulta that prints no date takes the PDF CreationDate (same plausibility window as the CENPROT reader), recorded as `emitida_em_origem='metadados_pdf'` and shown as inferred. A complete typed cell (resultado + número + emissão) counts as done. The party summary counts cells being read apart (`totais.processando` / `relendo`, "relendo n/N" with a progress bar; a re-read keeps the previous values visible), and "Editar" writes the saved row into the grid cache at once.
- **Certidões: the card tab owns every emission control** (the standalone /certidoes screen is being retired): per cell "Descartar emissão" (`DELETE /api/clientes/{id}/certidoes/resultados/{rid}`, soft-delete, the previous evidence wins again) and "Cancelar emissão" (`POST …/resultados/{rid}/cancelar`; per party `POST …/partes/{kind}/{alvo}/cancelar`; automatic consultas only, a manual cell is never cancelled); per party "Reemitir com erro" (`POST …/partes/{kind}/{alvo}/reemitir-erros`: only failed automatic cells, in place — same consulta, `erro`→`pendente`, TJSP→`na_fila`) and "Baixar todas (.zip)" (`GET …/download-zip`); "Solicitar emissão (todas)" skips cells already `sucesso` and in date or in emission (`ignoradas`, 409 `NADA_A_EMITIR` when nothing is left). All reuse `certidoes.service` (`cancelar_processamento`, `reprocessar_resultados`, `soft_delete_resultado`, `montar_zip`) and answer with the party as it reads afterwards. Cells carry `aguarda_humano` (the polling stop signal of an untouched manual placeholder — `origem` is now only who produced the values, so "Voltar à leitura automática" is no longer offered on an empty cell) and `emissao_automatica`; `totais.erros` feeds the "n com erro" summary and error text shows inline; "Negativa c/ homônimos" is its own chip.
- **Contract: Crednet companies and seller obligations** (migration 299, deal 857): a company read off a seller's Serasa Crednet enters the generated contract (with its certidões) only once someone confirms the participação on the card's Empresas section (`PATCH /api/clientes/{cliente_id}/empresas/{empresa_id}/participacao`, `confirmar | descartar | reabrir`; "Não é do vendedor" survives a Crednet re-read). Until then the readiness list names it as the non-blocking aviso `EMPRESA_CREDNET_NAO_CONFIRMADA`. The typed seller obligations print in the ÔNUS clause by default, or — per deal, `termos.obrigacoes_vendedor_local='certidoes'` — as one lettered paragraph "a) …; b) …." inside DAS CERTIDÕES E DOCUMENTOS, right after the seller's last person group (before any company group; no such group ⇒ before the imóvel's).

- **Imóvel ↔ condomínio** (migration 300, owner decision 2026-10-21): each imóvel is attached to ONE condomínio of the master list (`imovel_dados.condominio_id`, FK; a condomínio has many imóveis) or marked `casa_de_rua` — exclusive (CHECK + 422) and same-org (trigger); both probed by `verify_db_guards`. The property page's Cartório card and the "Cadastrar/Editar imóvel" modal share `CondominioSelector` (checkbox "Casa de rua", search-as-you-type over `GET /api/condominios/buscar?q=`, "Cadastrar condomínio “…”" via `POST /api/condominios`, deduped by normalised name, created `sugerido`). `em_condominio` (202) is legacy read-only — the API exposes it DERIVED (attached → true, casa de rua → false, neither → null). Contract: the attached condomínio's `nome` wins the title over `empreendimento_manual` and the CRM name; unanswered ⇒ readiness `falta` "Informe o condomínio do imóvel ou marque 'Casa de rua'" (alvo `imovel-condominio`); legacy "em condomínio" without a link ⇒ non-blocking aviso `CONDOMINIO_NAO_VINCULADO`. The `condominio_imoveis` view and the /condominios stats resolve the FK first, the name/alias match only for an unlinked, non-casa-de-rua imóvel; merging condomínios re-points attached imóveis (and the undo moves them back).

## Running

```bash
# Backend
uvicorn app.main:app --reload --port 8011 --app-dir products/social-wiring/backend

# Frontend (bare Vite dev — talks to a separately-run :8011 backend)
cd products/social-wiring/frontend && npm run dev
```

Same-origin under the single-container model: `VITE_BACKEND_API_URL` is runtime-detected (`src/lib/apiBase.ts`) and not baked. The boot-critical `VITE_SUPABASE_*` vars are Vite-inlined at `vite build` and asserted in `main.tsx`.

## Contract parties (matrícula-driven)

**Contract parties follow the matrícula.** A vendedor typed `proprietário` who is not among the matrícula's current owners (adquirentes of the last transfer act, `titulo_service.titularidade_atual`) blocks generation with `VENDEDOR_NAO_PROPRIETARIO` and the readiness names the right papel (cônjuge of an owner → anuente; doador in the chain → anuente); owners unknown checks nothing. When the sellers hold the imóvel by **doação** (adiantamento de legítima) the doadores are `anuente`s worded as such (`derivacao.doador_anuente`, no extra column); a spouse-anuente keeps its own wording. A CNH-only RG completes itself from the matrícula averbação (`card_hub/rg_matricula_service.py`, `rg_origem='matricula_averbacao'`), nacionalidade is inflected by gênero, and a certidão de casamento hands the linked cônjuge the couple's estado civil/regime/data.
