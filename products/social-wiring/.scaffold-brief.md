## What real-world domain does this product serve? (e.g. 'B2B real estate analytics', 'social media post scheduling', 'mental health therapy sessions')

Multi-channel media wiring & content management. A CMS that wires conversational AI (surface-agnostic OpenAI tool-orchestration chatbot reachable from WhatsApp via WAHA and a platform /chat page), Google (Calendar/Maps/Drive), Meta (Facebook/Instagram Graph, read), and YouTube (upload) into one place. Also houses two domains consolidated from now-retired products: email-marketing (from 'mailing') and real-estate media-crew scheduling (from 'imobi-scheduling').

## Who is the primary user? (e.g. 'real estate brokers', 'small business marketing teams', 'licensed therapists')

Operators and agencies managing social & media channels (e.g. real-estate agencies such as One Consultoria) — content managers, marketing teams, and scheduling coordinators who run everything through one conversational + dashboard surface.

## What are the 3-5 core entities/concepts? (e.g. for ERP: 'Imovel, Cliente, Negocio, Equipe')

Conversation/Message (durable, deduped), MediaAsset (audio/image/video/pdf, multimodal-resolved), Channel (WhatsApp/Meta/YouTube), CredentialConnection (OAuth/system-user, Fernet-encrypted), Campaign/Automation (email-marketing), Appointment (scheduling with crew + travel logic).

## Describe 1-3 primary workflows the product enables, end-to-end.

1) Inbound multimodal message (any channel) -> chatbot tool-orchestration loop -> action: answer / queue YouTube upload / Vista CRM lookup / read Drive content / schedule a calendar event. 2) Email-marketing: list -> dynamic segmentation -> campaign or multi-step automation -> send -> analytics + AI debrief. 3) Scheduling: WhatsApp request -> SchedulingEngine proposes slots (crew-skill + travel-buffer + lunch block) -> confirm -> Google Calendar event + appointment lifecycle with audit.

## External services this product integrates with (LLMs, APIs, webhooks, etc.). 'none' is fine.

OpenAI (chat completions, Whisper transcription, vision), WAHA (WhatsApp HTTP API), Meta Graph (Facebook + Instagram, read; system-user + user-oauth dual auth), Google Calendar/Maps/Drive (service-account + OAuth, scope auto-discovery), YouTube Data API (upload), Vista CRM, SMTP (email send), Redis (conversation buffer + dedup), Cloudflare quick-tunnel (profile-gated). All consumed from the noc seed (noctusai_lib / noctusai_seed) — the product wires, it does not reimplement.

## How will we know the product is doing its job? (key metrics, user outcomes)

Real-world functional at parity with the live-validated originating workspace; single-container house model; every cross-product integration consumed from seed (Protocol+Fake+Real+factory); backend pytest + vite build green; chatbot answers a realistic multimodal query end-to-end against Fakes in CI and real APIs in smoke.
