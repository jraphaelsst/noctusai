# ContentCard

An activity card on `sand`, under a section header with a "Ver todos →" link.

## Anatomy
- **Section header** — title in `h3` (Cormorant 500 23/28), "Ver todos →" in Inter 500 15 `stone`, baseline-aligned, max 380 wide.
- **Card** — `sand` ground, `radius-card` (16), padding 12, min height 128, 160–180 wide; a category chip (top-left), the title in `cardTitle` (Cormorant 600 20/24, `ink`), and a 30 × 30 `wine` arrow button bottom-right. Cards sit `space-12` apart.

## Rules
- Only `ink` text on sand.
- The consumer provides the title and category.
