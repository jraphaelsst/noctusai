# Accents

The collage vocabulary that sits behind and around the hero object: gold foil disc, torn navy slab, gold hairlines.

## Pieces
- **Foil disc** — `disc-d` (400-520px), `gold` with crinkle texture, `radius-disc`. One per post, center-right, behind the hero, partly cut by a slab or the hero.
- **Torn slab** — `slab` color, ragged edge with a `paper-shade` fibre lip. Either a vertical strip on the right edge or a floor plane across the bottom with a diagonal edge.
- **Gold hairlines** — `hairline` weight, `accent-line`: orbit ellipse or curved arrow around the hero, crop/registration lines, zig-zag downward trend arrow, kintsugi cracks on navy.
- **Rule** — see HeadlineStack.

## Rules
- At most two hairline motifs per post.
- Texture on every surface (grain at `grain` opacity). Nothing perfectly flat.
- The consumer provides the hero photo: grayscale, cut out, high contrast.
