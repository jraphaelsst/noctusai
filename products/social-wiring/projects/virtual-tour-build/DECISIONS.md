# Decisions log — Tours Virtuais

Rule: the owner decides every product question; nothing here is guessed. Technical decisions written by us are marked **proposed** until the owner or the tech-lead confirms them.

## Owner decisions — 2026-11-07 (binding for v1)

- **D-O1 Real estate only.** Construction is deferred until our app is 100% validated. The model stays open for it (`segmento` column; project/area added later), with no construction UI.
- **D-O2 360 source = upload** of equirectangular images from a 360 camera (Insta360/Ricoh, 2:1). Phone capture and stitching is a future phase. A `PanoramaSource` seam (`camera_360 | celular | fake`) lets it plug in later without changing v1; a single `ingest_equirect` path builds the variants (thumb, blurred preview, web).
- **D-O3 Navigation = pins only**: manual connections placed in both photos, with the arrival-angle convention studied in `[private study: specs/mechanisms.md]` §3.1. Arrows and circles (automatic from a floor plan) come later.
- **D-O4 Contact**: an always-visible WhatsApp/contact button in the viewer plus an optional lead form. Leads flow into social-wiring's EXISTING lead pipeline.
- **D-O5 Sharing**: a public link only. No portal embed and no per-portal stats yet (no ImovelWeb / Viva Real integration for this). The visit record must be able to carry a referrer later.
- **D-O6 MP4 auto-video**: later.
- **D-O7 Plan limit** unit = **tours per account**, never photos per tour. No per-tour photo limit.
- **D-O8 Fix what the reference gets wrong**: (a) concurrent editing must not be last-write-wins (optimistic versions or per-action saves); (b) an unpublished tour must never be publicly readable; (c) uploads must not be sequential.

## Technical decisions — proposed (2026-11-07)

- **D-T1 Tour ↔ imóvel by registry identity.** `tours_virtuais (org_id, codigo_imovel)` → FK `imovel_registry (org_id, codigo_canonical)`, the same shape as `atendimento_imoveis` (migration 181). Not `imoveis`: that table is a Vista mirror that shrinks when a listing leaves the catalog (migration 063 header), and a tour must survive a sale. `codigo_imovel` is required in v1 (see Q5 for a tour without a code).
- **D-T2 Normalised tables, no whole-document save.** Tour, photos, connections and hotspots are rows; every editor action is its own small endpoint (D-O8a). Each row carries `versao`; a write sends the version it read and gets **409 `versao_conflito`** (with the current row) when someone else changed it. Reorder sends the tour's version. The FE never retries a 409 silently: it refetches and tells the user.
- **D-T3 No static manifest.** The viewer manifest is built per request from the DB, only for a tour that is `publicado` AND not `removido`, reached through an active link. Unpublished, removed, revoked and unknown all answer the same 404 (no oracle). Panoramas sit in a **private** bucket and the manifest carries **short-TTL signed URLs** minted at read time; only storage paths are persisted (`KB § PATTERNS/backend/database-rls.md` § Storage buckets — never public). This closes D-O8b by construction.
- **D-T4 Parallel direct-to-storage upload.** The client asks for a signed upload URL per photo and PUTs the bytes straight to Supabase Storage (3 at a time by default), then confirms; a worker job ingests. Reason: an 8K equirect is 15–40 MB and a backend multipart upload goes through the Cloudflare origin's ~100 s cap (the incident behind the seed `prepareImageForUpload`, `seed/lib/frontend/src/imageUpload.ts`). Needs a seed storage extension (`signed_upload_url`, see `specs/sw-and-seed-map.md`). 🔴 The FE must NOT pass panoramas through `prepareImageForUpload` (it would shrink them to 3000 px).
- **D-T5 Angle convention (ours).** `yaw` ∈ (−π, π] around the vertical, `pitch` ∈ [−π/2, π/2] with 0 = horizon (the Photo Sphere Viewer convention, not the three.js polar angle). A connection is ONE row per pair of photos holding both pins. Arriving at photo B from photo A: `yaw = normalise(pin_B.yaw + π)`, `pitch = 0` — you face away from the door you came through, level. Per side, `usar_vista_inicial=true` lands on B's own start view instead. Keyed by photo **id**, never by URL, so replacing or re-processing a photo keeps its links.
- **D-T6 Viewer library: Photo Sphere Viewer v5 (MIT)**, three.js-based, with its markers, virtual-tour, autorotate and gyroscope plugins. No 360 viewer exists in the repo today (grep of every `package.json` and `seed/lib/frontend/src`, 2026-11-07). Alternatives: Pannellum (MIT, smaller, less active), Marzipano (Apache-2.0, little maintenance). Built as a seed FE organ `PanoramaViewer` so editor preview and public viewer share it. Licence and bundle size are re-verified in slice S3 before adoption.
- **D-T7 Floor plans: not in v1.** Pins need no plan; a plan only pays for itself with arrows/circles and a minimap (P2). No plan columns are added now (no unused schema); P2 adds `tour_plantas` and nullable placement columns on `tour_fotos`.
- **D-T8 Share link = an unguessable token** (`tour_links`, 128-bit, URL-safe), one active link per tour in v1, rotatable/revocable; `canal` column ready for per-portal links later (D-O5). Stored in clear because the editor must show it again; it grants read of published content only.
- **D-T9 Leads**: the viewer form calls the same path the portal ingests use, minus `registrar_imovel` (the tour's FK already guarantees the registry row) — `leads_service.create_lead` (which also fires the `lead_novo` notice) → `atendimento_imoveis_service.vincular_lead_seguro` — with a new canonical lead source `tour-virtual` (category `direto`) in `app/modules/leads/seed_data.py`. No new lead table; `tour_visitas.lead_id` links the visit.
- **D-T10 WhatsApp button** = `wa.me/<number>?text=<prefill naming the tour and the imóvel code>`. Number: the tour's own, else the org default (Q2). The click is recorded on the visit (`whatsapp_clicado`).
- **D-T11 Branding**: reuse the existing Branding/brand kits (`marcas`) for the viewer logo and colours instead of a separate "skin" entity. Q7 decides how far v1 goes.
- **D-T12 Seed-first split.** Seed: `noctusai_lib.integrations.panorama` (types, `PanoramaSource` Protocol + Fake + `Camera360Source` + factory, `ingest_equirect`), the imaging adapter extension (crop, blur, XMP read), the storage `signed_upload_url` extension, the FE organ `PanoramaViewer`. Product: tables, routes, screens. `PhoneStitchSource` is NOT stubbed in v1 (no placeholder Real; it ships whole in P3).
- **D-T13 Plan cap read through one seam** `TourQuota` (Protocol + Real + Fake). Real reads `public.plans.features->>'tours_virtuais_max'` through the org's active subscription; absent or null = unlimited. Counted: tours not `removido` (drafts count). Enforced on create and copy only; existing tours stay editable and visible at the cap. Pending Q4.
- **D-T14 Visit record, minimal and LGPD-light**: no IP, no cookie id, no user agent stored; `referrer_host` (host only) + `canal` columns exist from day one; device class, rooms seen, duration, WhatsApp click, lead id.

## Open — owner questions (blocking where marked)

1. **🔴 Access tier of the public viewer (blocking for S5/S7).** The public tier is a closed allowlist (`KB § PATTERNS/security/access-tiers.md`): landing, auth, pricing-checkout, legal-consent, lead-capture. A shared tour fits none. Options: (a) **tier `service`, signed capability link** — the handler verifies the token, like the e-mail double-opt-in links (`email_marketing/routers/confirm.py`) — recommended; (b) add a sixth public category such as `shared-listing`. The lead form on the viewer is either `service` under the same token (a) or `public/lead-capture` (b).
2. Default WhatsApp number: Julia's line (lead enters Julia automatically), the imóvel's corretor, or an org-wide number? Per-tour override allowed?
3. Lead form: fields (nome, telefone, e-mail?), trigger (button only, or also after N% of rooms seen — the reference got 18 leads in 2 years from its form), consent text. Is the source slug `tour-virtual` right?
4. Plan cap: needed in v1 (today the only user is the owner's org)? Where does the number live (proposed `plans.features.tours_virtuais_max`)? Do drafts count? Does removing a tour free a slot (proposed yes)?
5. One active tour per imóvel, or several (e.g. a re-shoot)? Allow a tour for an imóvel not yet in Vista (it would be registered in `imovel_registry` with the existing `origem_descoberta='manual'`, migration 063's CHECK — no new value needed)?
6. Who edits: every member, or creator + agency admins (the Edição de Fotos rule)? Who removes?
7. Branding v1: logo + colours from Marcas only, or also a nadir logo (hides the tripod) and autorotate settings per brand?
8. Info hotspots v1: text + link only (proposed), or also image/video?
9. Share URL: social-wiring's own host `/t/<token>` (proposed) or a branded domain / friendly slug?
10. Privacy: 360 photos show the whole room (family photos, documents, people). Is a manual blur tool needed before v1 goes live, or is it the photographer's job? Keep the full-resolution original (proposed: yes, private, for downloads and future re-processing)?
11. Upload ceiling: proposed 60 MB and 16384 × 8192 px per photo (covers 8K and 11K cameras). OK?
12. Visit analytics v1: count, duration, rooms seen and WhatsApp clicks only (proposed). Enough?
