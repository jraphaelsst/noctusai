### Recommendations for the next cycle

1. **Decide deliberately whether to restart.** The lead leg is now paused. If ONE10449 is still a
   priority listing, restart **once** with a plan and **don't touch it for 7 days**. Each pause or edit
   since 09-15 has reset learning.
2. **Restart with Larissa as the lead creative**, plus *one* challenger (Tour, R$ 175/lead). **Drop
   MESCLADO** (R$ 314, 0 leads) and **Carrossel** (R$ 382 per lead).
3. **Put Reels back, keep Stories out.** Reels produced leads at the cheapest CPM; Stories never did.
4. **Test excluding women 65+ and ages 25–34** in one variant, or at least a men-only vs all split.
   The evidence for this is stronger than on 10-06, though still small.
5. **Answer the 2 new Instagram comments on Tour.** Comments are rare on these posts, and these are
   real people.
6. **Review the instant form and join leads to the CRM.** These are still open from 10-06, and they will
   decide whether R$ 250–285 per lead is acceptable for an R$ 8M listing.
7. The traffic leg won't change by itself. Either give it its own ad set or accept that it is effectively
   off.

- **Attribution:** as in 10-06. Traffic uses a 1-day click window; leads use 7-day click or 1-day view.
  Don't add up results across the two legs.
- **Run #1's 10-06 values were partial-day.** The "since 10-06" numbers are run #2 minus run #1, so the
  rest of 10-06 counts as "since". Daily rows up to 10-05 were re-checked: **no retroactive changes**

- **10-14 is a partial day** (captured at ~15:20, ad set paused at 09:20). Leads can still be attributed
  to 10-07..10-14 clicks for up to 7 days, so re-check next week.
- **Correction to the 10-06 report:** it said quality rankings were UNKNOWN for every ad. Run #1 actually
  had *average* quality rankings for Tour, Carrossel and Larissa. Only engagement and conversion rankings
  were (and still are) unknown.

  pull does not expose "Aprendizado limitado" directly.

  `results` and `onsite_conversion.lead_grouped`, and they match at ad-set level (7).
- Meta changed result measurement on 2026-03-17. Don't compare against earlier periods.
