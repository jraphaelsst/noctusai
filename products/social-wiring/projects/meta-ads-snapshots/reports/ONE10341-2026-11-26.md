## 7. Recommendations

1. **Don't touch *Golden November*.** It is stable and has no fatigue. Avoid edits; each one triggers a
   re-review.
2. **Keep Stories in this property's mix.** It produces 94% of the leads at an average CPM. Don't apply
   ONE10449's "remove Stories" lesson here.
3. **Test a 35–64 age range**, or exclude men 65+. That is about R$ 65 a month with 0 leads.
4. **Test a stronger first second.** The hook is average (61% at 1 s). Reuse the opening style of ONE10449's
   relaunch ads (86–88% at 1 s) and compare in the same ad set.
5. **Check what "qualified" means in the CRM.** If the CRM sends `conversion_lead` at the "lead created"
   stage, Meta's QUALITY_LEAD optimization isn't learning anything about quality. Send a later stage
   (contact made, visit booked) instead.
6. **Watch the shared pool.** If ONE7312 or ONE6864 start winning the CBO budget, ONE10341's delivery will
   fall without any change on its own ad. The weekly report tracks its share of the ad set's spend.

## 8. Data caveats

- **"Qualified" = Meta's `conversion_leads` result**, which comes from CRM events sent back to Meta. That
  96% of form leads count as qualified suggests an early pipeline stage. Treat it as "CRM-acknowledged",
  not "qualified", until the CRM mapping is checked.
- **Shared ad sets:** ad-level numbers are exact for ONE10341. Ad set-level numbers (budget, learning)
  belong to the pool of 3 properties.
- **Window breakdowns** come from a `time_range` pull (10-27 → 11-26), not from lifetime.
- **Rankings** are known only where an ad had more than ~500 impressions; *Golden November* is ▲ quality.
- **11-26 is a partial day.**
