## 7. Diagnosis and recommendations

**Scorecard of the 10-14 recommendations**

| Recommendation | Done? | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Stop the stop-start; 7 uninterrupted days | ❌ Larissa v2 paused after 2 days, new ad on day 3 | Learning reset again |
| Keep the best creative (Larissa) running | ⚠️ Remade as Larissa v2, then paused | Lost the only converting ad |
| Bring Reels back | ❌ Still Feed only | CPM R$ 83 |
| Join leads to the CRM | ❌ Not yet | Lead quality still unknown |

**Recommendations**
1. **Reactivate Larissa v2 and freeze the ad set for 7 days.** It has the best retention and the only lead.
   Run at most 3 ads and make no edits.
2. **Add Instagram Reels back** (keep Stories off). Reels was the cheapest placement and produced leads.
   Expect CPM to fall from about R$ 83 toward R$ 40–50.
3. **Cap age at 54**, or run a 25–54 vs 55+ test. 55+ costs 2–4× per impression with no leads, either now
   or in lifetime data.
4. **Fix the end of the videos.** The hook works, but link CTR is 0,15–0,64%. Add a spoken and on-screen
   call to action ("cadastre-se para ver a planta / agendar visita") in the first 8 seconds, where 17–27%
   of viewers are still watching.
5. **Compare CPL on one attribution window.** The ad set moved to 1-day click. Either switch back to
   7-day click / 1-day view or report both windows; the store already keeps both.
6. **Join leads to the CRM**, so the next report measures cost per qualified lead or visit. For an
   R$ 8M property, form CPL alone says little.

**Next report:** 2026-12-03, run #4, measured after 7 untouched days if recommendation 1 is applied.

## 8. Data caveats

- **`results` / `cost_per_result` are missing for lead ads in the API pull** (system-user token). Leads here
  come from `onsite_conversion.lead_grouped`, the canonical count. `lead` matched it for every ad in this
  run.
- **`video_continuous_2_sec_watched_actions` is silently dropped by Graph v22**, so 2-second views are not
  available. Use curve second 2 instead.
- **Breakdowns are lifetime** for the selected ads. Section 6 filters to the 4 relaunch ads, so it covers
  11-23 → 11-26 only.
- **Rankings are UNKNOWN** for every lead ad (under ~500 impressions per ad).
- **Small numbers:** the relaunch has 1 lead. Every conclusion about leads is a hypothesis. The retention
  and CTR conclusions rest on thousands of impressions and are firmer.
- **11-26 is a partial day** (pulled at ~14:00).
