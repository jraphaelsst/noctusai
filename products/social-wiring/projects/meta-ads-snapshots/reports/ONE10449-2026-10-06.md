## 7. Diagnosis and recommendations

1. **Stop the stop-start.** Each pause or edit resets learning. Give one lead ad set at least
   **7 uninterrupted days** before judging it, and don't edit targeting after launch.
2. **Consolidate creatives inside one stable ad set** (MESCLADO plus the best performer, Larissa,
   at R$ 159 per lead) rather than launching and pausing them one after another.
3. **Test removing Instagram Stories** from the lead ad set, or at least compare it against Feed + Reels.
4. **Revive the traffic leg properly.** In the shared pool, ONE10449 now loses the auction for budget to
   the other properties' ads. If profile traffic for this listing matters, give it its own ad set or
   a minimum spend.
5. **Fix the form step** (98% drop-off). Review the instant-form length and questions; the
   higher-intent form type is what costs R$ 222 per lead.
6. **Join leads to the CRM** (social-wiring already ingests Meta leads, `033_meta_ads_leads.sql`), so
   that the next report measures **cost per qualified lead or visit**, not cost per form.

## 8. Data caveats

- **Attribution:** "Resultados" uses each ad set's own window (traffic: 1-day click; leads: 7-day click
  or 1-day view). One Tour lead was view-through only. Never add up results across the two legs as if
  they were the same metric.
- **`actions:lead` double-counts** (form plus pixel). Lead numbers here come from `results`
  (`leadgen.other`) / `onsite_conversion.lead_grouped`.
- **Reach is not additive** across ads or days. The property-level reach is a lower bound
  (max = 15.690).
- **Quality / engagement / conversion rankings are UNKNOWN** for every ad (too few impressions).
- **Meta changed result measurement on 2026-03-17** (banner in Ads Manager). Don't compare against
  periods before that.
- **MESCLADO is live**, so its numbers moved during capture (R$ 146,62 → R$ 147,40). Daily rows for the
  last 7 days can still change while attribution windows close; weekly snapshots re-capture them.

  retention · 6 Audience (`insight_breakdown`) · 7 Recommendations · 8 Caveats.

  whether the numbers are property-exclusive.
