## What real-world domain does this product serve? (e.g. 'B2B real estate analytics', 'social media post scheduling', 'mental health therapy sessions')

YouTube channel content management and analytics — uploading videos to YouTube, notifying contacts when new videos publish, and tracking channel metrics

## Who is the primary user? (e.g. 'real estate brokers', 'small business marketing teams', 'licensed therapists')

Solo creators and small content teams managing a single YouTube channel — people who want a lightweight cockpit to upload (from browser or Google Drive link), auto-notify their audience, and watch channel analytics without opening YouTube Studio

## What are the 3-5 core entities/concepts? (e.g. for ERP: 'Imovel, Cliente, Negocio, Equipe')

Channel, Video, UploadJob, NotificationRecipient, Credential (encrypted YouTube OAuth tokens), NotificationLog

## Describe 1-3 primary workflows the product enables, end-to-end.

1) OAuth-connect a YouTube channel via Settings, then upload a video either by drag-drop or by pasting a Google Drive shared link — backend downloads from Drive and uploads to YouTube; 2) On successful upload, dispatch notifications via WhatsApp (WAHA) and Email (SMTP) to the curated recipient list (per-video deselect supported); 3) Browse the entire channel inventory with cached metrics (views, likes, comments) and force-sync from the YouTube Data API; 4) Dashboard surfaces KPI cards, daily-views chart, top-5 videos, and recent uploads with notification-delivery status

## External services this product integrates with (LLMs, APIs, webhooks, etc.). 'none' is fine.

YouTube Data API v3 (OAuth 2.0 — youtube.upload + youtube.readonly + youtube.force-ssl scopes; resumable uploads; quota-aware batching), Google Drive (httpx + gdown for downloading shared links), WAHA (WhatsApp HTTP API — consumed via noctusai_lib.integrations.whatsapp seed factory; FakeWahaClient fallback when unconfigured), SMTP (Gmail App Password via smtplib.SMTP_SSL), Supabase (Postgres + RLS), Redis (upload-job tracking + caching), Cryptography Fernet (encrypts refresh tokens at rest in DB)

## How will we know the product is doing its job? (key metrics, user outcomes)

Upload success rate ≥99% for files under 5GB; notification delivery rate ≥95% across both channels; dashboard metrics within 1% of YouTube Studio numbers; full channel sync completes in under 60s for channels with <500 videos; YouTube API quota usage stays under 5,000 units/day on typical workloads (10k daily quota with comfortable headroom)

## Naming conventions specific to this product's domain? (e.g. 'use Portuguese for entities, English for code')

English for code, entities, and database identifiers (Video, UploadJob, NotificationRecipient). User-facing UI strings in Portuguese (matching noc platform convention — Configuracoes / Equipe / Videos). YouTube API field names preserved as-is (snake_case from API responses) on the boundary, mapped to Python attributes inside the service layer.
